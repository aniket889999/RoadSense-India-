# ADR 0002: Temporal & Spatial Clustering for Road Event Deduplication

## Status
Accepted

## Context
When a vehicle drives over or past a pothole at 30 km/h, the detector may fire across 5 to 20 consecutive video frames. Treating every bounding box as an individual pothole would severely inflate incident counts, overwhelm human reviewers, and produce duplicate repair work-orders.

However, single-camera dashcam video without verified high-precision RTK-GPS cannot claim true physical-world object identity.

## Decision
We establish a two-tiered data model:
1. `RawDetection`: Represents a single 2D bounding box generated by the YOLOv8n detector on a specific frame index.
2. `RoadEvent`: Represents a temporal cluster of continuous raw detections proposed for human review.

### Grouping Rules:
- **Temporal Window**: Detections must occur within $\Delta t \le 1.5\text{ seconds}$ of the cluster's latest detection.
- **Spatial Continuity**: Detections must satisfy either:
  - Intersection over Union ($\text{IoU} \ge 0.15$) with the previous detection box, OR
  - Centroid distance $\le 80\text{ pixels}$ in normalized coordinate space.
- **Representative Detection**: The detection with the highest confidence score within the cluster is selected as the representative evidence thumbnail.
- **Review Status**: The proposed `RoadEvent` starts in `PENDING_REVIEW` and can be transitioned to `CONFIRMED`, `REJECTED`, or `NEEDS_REVISIT` only by explicit human reviewer action.

## Consequences
- Prevents 10-fold overcounting of the same physical road feature.
- Transparent heuristics avoid false claims of GPS triangulation or 3D object tracking.
